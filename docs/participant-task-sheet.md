# Try NASA Mission Control

Thank you for helping us test the app. We are testing the website, not your space knowledge. No account is needed. Use your own familiar phone, tablet, or computer and browser. Allow about 20–30 minutes. You may skip a task or stop at any time.

Open [NASA Mission Control](https://nasa-mission-control-alpha.vercel.app/). Please try the tasks below on your own, without hints from the observer. If something is confusing, say what you expected to happen. You do not need to guess what the designer intended.

1. Find NASA's astronomy selection for January 1, 2024. Say which date the page is showing, then save that selection so you can find it later.
2. Find a mission that went to Mars and open its record. Then return to the full mission list.
3. Find the astronomy selection you saved earlier and reopen it. Where would you look for it on a later visit?
4. Try a space quiz question. Say what the feedback means, then continue to the next question.

If a task seems impossible, stop and tell the observer where you got stuck. A wrong quiz answer is not a failed task. NASA data can sometimes be unavailable; report any error you encounter.

After the tasks, tell the observer:

- What was hardest to find or understand?
- Did anything behave differently from what you expected?
- What one change would make this easier to use?

Please do not share personal information, passwords, or private browser content. Nothing needs to be purchased or installed. The observer should ask before taking notes or making a recording. Saved items stay in this browser; do not clear existing browser data for this test.
