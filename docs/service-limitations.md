# Service limitations

- This is an educational portfolio project, not an official NASA product,
  spacecraft control system, hazard warning service, or operational forecast.
- APOD, NeoWs, DONKI, EPIC, GIBS, and the NASA Image and Video Library are
  independent public upstreams with different latency, availability, quotas,
  update schedules, and historical coverage.
- “Live” means recently retrieved from an upstream; it does not mean a
  real-time spacecraft feed. “Latest available” may be delayed.
- DONKI is preliminary research information. NOAA SWPC remains the linked
  operational space-weather source.
- Curated missions and stories are reviewed snapshots. Scheduled link checks do
  not replace human fact and status review.
- Local calculations are explanatory approximations with stated inputs and
  reference frames, not trajectory or risk models.
- The Flight Log is local to one browser. There is no account sync, recovery
  service, server copy, or guarantee that browser storage will persist. Users
  must export backups for portability.
- The offline shell includes curated routes and previously used static assets;
  live `/api` data is deliberately never cached by the service worker.
- Automated accessibility and deterministic browser checks do not prove
  screen-reader usability or comprehension. No multi-participant or completed
  manual screen-reader sample is currently claimed.
- Reliability and route-performance history has not yet accumulated a complete
  representative 30-day window, and the JavaScript build currently passes its
  aggregate ceiling without rounded headroom.
- GitHub-scheduled monitoring can be delayed and is not an SLA or 24/7 pager.
